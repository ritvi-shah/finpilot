
# FinPilot — 60-second pitch

People have financial data everywhere: bank statements, card statements, bills, subscriptions and spreadsheets. The problem is not a lack of data. The problem is that the data is disconnected.

FinPilot turns those records into one explainable financial workspace.

A user uploads a transaction statement. FinPilot categorizes transactions, detects recurring payments, identifies unusual spending signals, summarizes monthly cash flow, tracks upcoming bills, compares budgets with actual spending, connects spending with savings goals and answers natural-language questions.

The important part is the agent flow: it observes the user's records, finds patterns, explains the evidence and creates a prioritized action queue.

For example, instead of only saying "Food increased," FinPilot can compare months, show the amount and percentage change, identify the underlying transactions, and surface it as something to review.

The system is deliberately reliable for a hackathon: core calculations run locally without bank credentials, paid APIs or live financial feeds.

FinPilot does not give investment advice. It helps people understand their everyday financial information and make their own informed decisions.
